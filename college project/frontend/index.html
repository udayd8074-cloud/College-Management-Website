About Our College

Future Tech College offers BA, B.Sc., B.Com., BCA, BBA, and postgraduate courses in English/Commerce. Facilities include modern laboratories, a digital library, virtual/smart classrooms, a playground, and Wi-Fi (especially for BCA/BBA students).

About Us

Future Tech College

Established in 1995, Future Tech College has been at the forefront of education in the region. Our college offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs in various departments.

We are committed to providing our students with a holistic education that combines theoretical knowledge with practical skills. Our campus features modern laboratories, a well-stocked library, sports facilities, and comfortable hostel accommodations.

Our alumni have excelled in various fields and are working in top companies around the world. We continue to strive for excellence in education, research, and innovation.
